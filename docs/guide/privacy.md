# Privacy

Verbatim is built so your voice never leaves your device.

- **On-device transcription.** Audio is captured and transcribed locally by models running on your machine. Nothing is uploaded to a server.
- **No account, no telemetry.** There is no sign-in, and the app collects no usage analytics.
- **Works offline.** Once a model is downloaded, Verbatim needs no network connection.
- **Open source.** You can read exactly what the app does — [browse the source](https://github.com/GalaxyRuler/Verbatim).

## What is stored locally

- Downloaded models, cached on disk so they aren't re-downloaded.
- Your settings (hotkey, selected model, preferences).

Transcribed text is inserted into the app you're using and stays on your device.
