# Getting started

Once Verbatim is installed:

1. **Launch Verbatim.** It runs in the background, accessible from the tray (Windows/Linux) or menu bar (macOS).
2. **Download a model.** On first run, pick and download a speech model — see [Models](models.md). Smaller models are faster; larger ones are more accurate.
3. **Set your hotkey.** In Settings, assign a global hotkey for dictation. It works system-wide, in any app.
4. **Dictate.** Put your cursor in any text field, trigger your hotkey, speak, and stop. Verbatim transcribes on your device and types the text where the cursor is.

## Tips

- Speak at a natural pace — no need to over-enunciate.
- The first transcription after launch can take a moment while the model loads into memory; later ones are faster.
- Everything runs locally. After the model is downloaded, no internet connection is required.
